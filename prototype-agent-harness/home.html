01 — HOW A PURCHASE HAPPENS

THREE MOVES. THEN A TAP.

01 They say it

Plain words, in English or Georgian. “Two shawarmas, no onion.” “A room in Vake this weekend.” Voice or typing, on your site.

02 We find it

The agent searches only your catalog, near the customer. Prices, fees and options come from you. The model cannot invent a price.

03 They tap

A card shows the place, the lines and the total. Confirm & pay is theirs. Until that tap, the order is only a proposal.

02 — THE RULE

THE AGENT CANNOT PRESS PAY.

AWAITING THEIR TAP

This is the whole safety story. Software can prepare a checkout. A person moves the money.

  • 01
    The agent can create a payment card. Confirm and cancel come from the customer’s tap, never from the model.
  • 02
    Prices are read from your catalog. A wrong item is rejected. One order is one venue, one store, one flight, one ride.
  • 03
    You set a per-order ceiling. Over the limit, the agent must change the order or refuse it.
  • 04
    Memory stays inside your company. The same person on another brand is a stranger. Preferences do not cross.
  • 05
    An unpaid card expires. A new proposal replaces the old one. Nothing sits open in the background.

06 — WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

FOUR THINGS. THEN IT IS YOURS.

CATALOG

Products or places, with ids, prices and options. A file is enough to start. Later, your live menu, inventory or booking API.

BRAND

Name, color, currency, languages, and the maximum one order may reach.

PAYER

The card or wallet already on the customer’s account. We propose. Your payment provider charges when they confirm.

WHO IS ASKING

Your backend tells us which logged-in customer this is. The public key in the page only names your company.

FOR YOUR DEVELOPERS

ONE KEY. ONE TURN.

Every call carries x-agentpay-key. The key chooses the catalog, the memory and the limit. A turn streams four kinds of events, then stops on the card.

statusShort lines while it works. “Searching your catalog.”
toolWhat it looked up. Search, menu, a payment proposal, a saved preference.
paymentThe card. Status stays awaiting_user_confirmation until the tap.
replyThe sentence the customer reads.

Confirm is a separate call: POST /v1/payments/:id/confirm. The model is not allowed to make it.
